# NeuroSyn — Technical Digital PR & Content Syndication Playbook

Syndicating NeuroSyn's 8 published technical papers onto high-authority developer platforms (**Dev.to**, **Medium**, **HackerNoon**, **Hashnode**) is the most effective way to earn tier-1 backlinks without risking Google duplicate content penalties.

---

## ⚡ The Golden Rule: Always Use `rel="canonical"`

When publishing on external platforms, you **MUST** specify the original canonical URL pointing back to `https://www.neurosyn.it.com/insights/...`. This tells Googlebot:
> *"The true original authoritative source is NeuroSyn. Transfer all domain equity and backlink PageRank to neurosyn.it.com."*

---

## 🚀 Platform-by-Platform Instructions

### 1. Dev.to (Domain Authority: 92)
Dev.to supports markdown frontmatter with native canonical tags.

When creating a new post on Dev.to, paste this frontmatter at the very top:

```markdown
---
title: "How Private AI Search Works on Company Documents, and When It Beats a Chatbot"
published: true
description: "How private enterprise AI search indexes internal corporate files with access controls, permissions, and grounded citations."
tags: ai, architecture, enterprise, programming
canonical_url: "https://www.neurosyn.it.com/insights/private-ai-search-company-documents"
---
```

### 2. Medium (Domain Authority: 95)
Medium has a dedicated canonical attribution feature:
1. Go to **Write a story** (or use the Medium "Import a story" tool with your live URL).
2. Click the three dots `...` in the top right > **More settings**.
3. Scroll down to **Advanced Settings**.
4. Check the box: *"This story was originally published elsewhere"*.
5. Paste the exact NeuroSyn URL:
   `https://www.neurosyn.it.com/insights/<slug>`
6. Click **Save Canonical URL** and publish.

### 3. HackerNoon (Domain Authority: 88)
1. Submit your draft through the HackerNoon writer dashboard.
2. Under **Article Settings**, find the **Canonical Link** field.
3. Input:
   `https://www.neurosyn.it.com/insights/<slug>`
4. Mention NeuroSyn Research in the bio.

### 4. Hashnode (Domain Authority: 85)
1. Create a post in the Hashnode editor.
2. In the right-hand **Article Settings** drawer, scroll to **Advanced Settings**.
3. Under **Original Article URL**, paste:
   `https://www.neurosyn.it.com/insights/<slug>`
